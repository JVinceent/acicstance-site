Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-09

Who we are

aCICStance is a student-run service of the College of Informatics and Computing Sciences, Batangas State University – The National Engineering University, Alangilan Campus. This notice explains how the aCICStance app handles your personal data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Your name, SR Code, section, program, and school email address.
  • If you sign in with Google: only your name and school email address from your Google account. We never see your Google password or anything else in your account.
  • A referral code you enter, if any.
  • The requests you make (for example printing, supplies, hygiene kits), their status, and the files you upload for printing.
  • Your aCICSt Points history and any feedback or ratings you submit.
  • Basic technical information when the app crashes, without your name or SR Code.

Why we collect it

  • To confirm you are a BatStateU student and let you sign in.
  • To process your requests and tell you when they are ready.
  • To give and track aCICSt Points and referral rewards.
  • To forward concerns you submit to the right College or University office.
  • To produce usage reports. Reports shared outside aCICStance, including with partners, use only totals that do not identify you.

Who can see it

You can see your own data. Authorized aCICStance officers can see what they need to fulfil requests. Feedback you submit may be forwarded to the College or University office responsible for it. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Files uploaded for printing are deleted about 7 days after your request is finished. Requests, points, and feedback are kept for the current and previous school year, then anonymized. You may ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to see, correct, or delete your personal data, and you may withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent means closing your account. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Contact

Questions or requests: email acicstance.corner@gmail.com, or visit the aCICStance Corner at CICS, BatStateU Alangilan.
