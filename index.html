aCICStance

Student services from the aCICStance Corner — College of Informatics and Computing Sciences, Batangas State University – The National Engineering University, Alangilan Campus.

What the app does

  • Free printing and binding requests, with a live queue
  • School supplies, hygiene kits and borrowable items
  • Reviewer hub, tutoring and academic-contest support
  • DRRM assistance, events and FAQs
  • aCICSt Points rewards, feedback and referrals

Who can use it

BatStateU students sign in with their school account (@g.batstate-u.edu.ph) — with Google or with their SR Code and password. Guests can browse.
